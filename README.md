## 📌 About The Project

NEWS is a modern and responsive News & Magazine website designed to provide readers with an engaging platform to explore headline stories, featured articles, latest news, and trending topics.

The website offers a clean and attractive interface with organized news categories, article sections, author information, and a sidebar to improve the overall reading experience.

## ✨ Features

- 🏠 **Home Page** – Displays top headlines and important stories.
- 🚨 **Breaking News** – Highlights important news updates.
- 🗂️ **News Categories** – Explore different categories of news.
- ⭐ **Featured Articles** – Showcases interesting and important articles.
- 📰 **Latest News** – Displays recent news updates.
- 🔥 **Trending News** – Highlights popular stories.
- ✍️ **Author Information** – Displays writer and editorial details.
- 📌 **Sidebar Section** – Provides additional news and information.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Compatible with desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- 🎨 **Modern UI** – Attractive colors, typography, and organized layouts.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – For webpage structure.
- **CSS3** – For styling and visual design.
- **CSS Grid** – For structured content layouts.
- **Flexbox** – For flexible alignment.
- **Media Queries** – For responsive design.
